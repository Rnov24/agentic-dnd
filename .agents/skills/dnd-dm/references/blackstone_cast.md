# Blackstone Fortress Cast Reference

## Aria Nightwind (Player Character)
- **Class**: Level 3 Rogue (Thief)
- **Demeanor**: Pragmatic, stealthy, distrusting of authority, quick-witted.

## Eldrin Shadowseeker (AI Companion)
- **Class**: Level 3 Wizard (Evocation)
- **Demeanor**: Analytical, cautious, fascinated by arcane antiquities, speaks in articulate cadences.
- **Role**: Offers tactical magical support (e.g. *Minor Illusion*, *Fire Bolt*, *Mage Armor*).

## Guard Karl (Cellblock Sentry)
- **Role**: Prison guard holding the cellblock keyring.
- **Personality**: Sluggish, tired, easily distracted by unexpected noises.
- **Voice**: Gruff, raspy, mutters under his breath.

## Valen the Scholar (Prisoner in Cell #3)
- **Role**: Scholar holding cipher notes stolen by the garrison.
- **Personality**: Frail, frightened, immensely grateful if rescued.

## Captain Aldric (Garrison Commander)
- **Role**: Fortress commander and high-threat adversary.
- **Personality**: Relentless, fanatical, tactical genius.
- **Voice**: Deep, authoritative, uncompromising.
