# Sensory Vocabulary & Atmospheric Palette

Use this vocabulary to evoke visceral Theater-of-the-Mind descriptions.

## 1. Dungeon & Fortress Environments
- **Lighting**: Flickering sconce, sputtering torch, sickly phosphorescence, oppressive gloom, moonlit silhouette, pooling amber radiance.
- **Acoustics**: Rhythmic water droplets, echoing iron grates, grinding hinges, muffled pacing, distant thunder rolls, whistling drafts.
- **Olfactory**: Damp straw, ozone from spent sorcery, rusted iron, sulfur, cold stone dust, stagnant bilge.
- **Tactile**: Chilled drafts, slick moss, gritty stone mortar, freezing condensation, heavy iron keys.

## 2. Combat & Spellcasting Sensations
- **Blade Impact**: Obsidian shearing leather, metallic ringing, dull thud of heavy mail, slicing breeze.
- **Magic**: Crackle of raw evocation, air smelling of charred hair, hair standing on end, humming glyphs, azure arcane sparks.
- **Tension**: Heart hammering in the throat, breath catching in the chest, white-knuckle grip, silence before the alarm.
