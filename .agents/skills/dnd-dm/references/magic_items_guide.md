# Magic Items & Attunement Guide (D&D Basic Rules)

## 1. Magic Item Discovery & Identification
- Magic items can be identified over a Short Rest or via *Identify* / *Detect Magic*.
- Display magic item properties via:
  ```bash
  python dnd.py item <item_name>
  ```

## 2. Attunement Guidelines
- Characters can be attuned to a maximum of **3 items** simultaneously.
- Attunement takes 1 uninterrupted Short Rest.
- Items requiring attunement: *Amulet of Health, Cloak of Elvenkind, Gauntlets of Ogre Power, Headband of Intellect, Ring of Protection, Ring of Resistance*.
- Items not requiring attunement: *Bag of Holding, Goggles of Night, Wand of Magic Missiles, Weapon +1, Armor +1, Potions*.

## 3. Reward Distribution by Tier
- **Tier 1 (Levels 1-4)**: Common potions, spell scrolls, minor wondrous items (Bag of Holding, Goggles of Night), +1 weapons.
- **Tier 2 (Levels 5-10)**: Rare armor/weapons (+2), Cloak of Elvenkind, Amulet of Health, Ring of Protection.
