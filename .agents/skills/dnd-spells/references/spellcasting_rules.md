# 2024 Spellcasting Rules & Upcasting Reference

## Concentration Mechanics
- **Incapacitated**: Concentration ends immediately if you are Incapacitated or killed.
- **Casting another concentration spell**: The previous spell ends instantly.
- **Damage**: Roll Constitution save vs $\max(10, \lfloor \text{Damage}/2 \rfloor)$ for each instance of damage taken.

## Upcasting Calculations
- **Damage Scaling**: Many spells gain +1 damage die per level above base (e.g. *Magic Missile* adds +1 dart per slot level above 1st).
- **Target Scaling**: Spells like *Hold Person* target +1 additional humanoid within 30ft per slot level above 2nd.
