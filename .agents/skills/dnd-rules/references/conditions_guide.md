# D&D 5e (2024) Conditions Reference

- **Blinded**: Can't see; auto-fails sight checks; attacks vs have ADV, own attacks have DISADV.
- **Charmed**: Can't attack the charmer; charmer has ADV on social checks.
- **Deafened**: Can't hear; auto-fails hearing checks.
- **Exhaustion (2024)**: Levels 1-6. Each level subtracts -2 from all d20 tests and -5ft speed. Level 6 is death.
- **Frightened**: DISADV on checks and attacks while fear source is visible; cannot move closer.
- **Grappled**: Speed 0.
- **Incapacitated**: Can't take actions, bonus actions, or reactions. Concentration broken.
- **Invisible**: Unseen; attacks against have DISADV; own attacks have ADV.
- **Paralyzed**: Incapacitated, speed 0; auto-fails STR/DEX saves; attacks vs have ADV; hits within 5ft are crits.
- **Petrified**: Transformed into stone; Incapacitated; resistant to all damage; immune to poison.
- **Poisoned**: DISADV on attack rolls and ability checks.
- **Prone**: Must spend half speed to stand; melee attacks vs have ADV; ranged attacks vs have DISADV; own attacks have DISADV.
- **Restrained**: Speed 0; attacks vs have ADV; own attacks have DISADV; DISADV on DEX saves.
- **Stunned**: Incapacitated; can't move; auto-fails STR/DEX saves; attacks vs have ADV.
- **Unconscious**: Incapacitated, prone, drops items; auto-fails STR/DEX saves; hits within 5ft are crits.
