# D&D 5e (2024) Weapon Mastery Reference

| Mastery | Weapons | Trigger Condition | Mechanical Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cleave** | Greataxe, Halberd | On melee hit | Attack second creature within 5ft for weapon die damage without ability mod |
| **Graze** | Greatsword, Glaive | On weapon attack miss | Target takes damage equal to ability modifier (minimum 1) |
| **Nick** | Dagger, Scimitar, Light Hammer | On Light weapon attack | Extra attack occurs within the Attack action, freeing up your Bonus Action |
| **Push** | Warhammer, Pike, Heavy Crossbow | On attack hit | Target is pushed up to 10ft straight back if Large or smaller |
| **Sap** | Flail, Morningstar, Mace | On attack hit | Target suffers Disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn |
| **Slow** | Longbow, Whip, Club, Javelin | On hit and damage | Target speed reduced by 10ft until start of your next turn |
| **Topple** | Battleaxe, Lance, Trident, Quarterstaff | On attack hit | Target must pass CON save (DC 8 + Mod + Prof) or fall Prone |
| **Vex** | Rapier, Shortbow, Shortsword, Hand Crossbow | On hit and damage | Gain Advantage on your next attack roll against this target |
