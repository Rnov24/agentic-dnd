# Part 3: The Spider's Web (Levels 3–4)

## Overview
The party explores the wilderness of the Triboar region to track down Cragmaw Castle, find Gundren Rockseeker, and locate Wave Echo Cave.

## Key Wilderness Sites
1. **Ruins of Conyberry & Agatha's Lair**: Home to the banshee Agatha. If approached respectfully with Garaele's gift, she answers one question truthfully (e.g. location of Bowgentle's spellbook or Wave Echo Cave).
2. **Old Owl Well**: Ancient Netherese watchtower ruins. Hamun Kost, a Red Wizard of Thay necromancer, digs with 12 obedient zombies.
3. **Wyvern Tor**: A cave inhabited by Brughor Axe-Biter, 6 orcs, and 1 ogre raiding local travelers.
4. **Ruins of Thundertree**: Destroyed by Mount Hotenow's eruption. Inhabited by Ash Zombies, Twig Blights, Reidoth the druid, Dragon Cultists led by Favric, and **Venomfang**, a proud Young Green Dragon nesting in the wizard's tower.
5. **Cragmaw Castle**: Stronghold of the Cragmaw goblins:
   - 7 keyed areas defended by hobgoblins, gricks, and goblins.
   - King Grol the bugbear, Snarl the wolf, and **Vyerith**, a doppelganger disguised as a drow envoy.
   - Gundren Rockseeker is held hostage; his map to Wave Echo Cave is hidden under Grol's bed.
