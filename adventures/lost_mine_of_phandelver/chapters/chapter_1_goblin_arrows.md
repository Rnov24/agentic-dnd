# Part 1: Goblin Arrows (Level 1)

## Overview
The adventure begins on the High Road from Neverwinter. Gundren Rockseeker hired the party to escort a wagon of supplies to Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin for 10 gp per character. Gundren and his escort, Sildar Hallwinter, rode ahead on horses.

## Key Encounters & Locations
1. **Goblin Ambush**: 2 dead horses (belonging to Gundren and Sildar) block the road. 4 Cragmaw goblins ambush the party from thickets. One horse saddlebag has an empty leather map case.
2. **Goblin Trail**: A concealed trail leading 5 miles northwest to the Cragmaw Hideout. Contains two traps:
   - *Snare Trap*: DC 12 Perception to spot; DC 10 DEX save or suspended upside down.
   - *Pit Trap*: 10-ft-deep pit concealed by leaves; DC 15 Perception to spot; DC 10 DEX save or fall taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
3. **Cragmaw Hideout**:
   - **Cave Mouth**: Stream flowing out, briars concealing entrance.
   - **Kennel**: 3 chained wolves. Can be calmed with DC 15 Animal Handling.
   - **Overpass & Flood Trap**: Goblins can release water reservoirs causing a DC 15 DEX save or swept away for 1d6 damage.
   - **Goblin Den**: Yeemik and 5 goblins holding Sildar Hallwinter hostage (at 3 HP). Yeemik demands Klarg's death to release Sildar.
   - **Klarg's Cave**: Klarg the bugbear, Ripper the wolf, 2 goblins, and stolen crates with Lionshield Coster insignia.
