# Part 2: Phandalin (Levels 2–3)

## Town Overview
Phandalin is a burgeoning frontier boomtown built atop the ruins of the old settlement. It has no formal government, only a cowardly elected townmaster, Harbin Wester.

## Key Town Establishments & NPCs
- **Stonehill Inn**: Run by Toblen Stonehill. Warm hearth, central gossip hub.
- **Barthen's Provisions**: Run by Elmar Barthen. Delivers the supply wagon for 50 gp reward.
- **Lionshield Coster**: Run by Linene Graywind. Offers 50 gp reward for stolen crates recovered from Cragmaw Hideout.
- **Phandalin Miner's Exchange**: Run by Halia Thornton (secret Zhentarim agent). Offers 100 gp for killing Glasstaff and retrieving his correspondence.
- **Shrine of Luck**: Sister Garaele (Harpers contact). Asks the party to consult Agatha the banshee with an ornate silver comb.
- **Alderleaf Farm**: Qelline Alderleaf and her adventurous boy Carp, who found a secret tunnel into the Redbrand Hideout.
- **Townmaster's Hall**: Harbin Wester (offers 100 gp bounty for Wyvern Tor orcs) and Sildar Hallwinter (offers 500 gp to find Wave Echo Cave).
- **Sleeping Giant Taphouse**: Rough dive where Redbrand Ruffians loiter.

## Redbrand Hideout (Tresendar Manor Cellars)
12 keyed areas beneath the ruined manor:
- Trapped hallways, crypts with 3 skeletons, slave pens with Mirna Dendrar and children, an underground crevasse inhabited by a telepathic Nothic, bugbear guards sent by the Black Spider, and the quarters of Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek with his *Staff of Defense*.
