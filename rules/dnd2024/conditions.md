# D&D 5e (2024 Revision) Conditions Compendium

Comprehensive, definitive condition reference based on the official 2024 Player's Handbook (Appendix C: Rules Glossary).

| Condition | Core Mechanical Effects (2024 Revision) |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | **Can't See**: Auto-fail any ability check requiring sight.<br>**Attacks Affected**: Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and your attack rolls have Disadvantage. |
| **Charmed** | **Can't Harm Charmer**: Cannot attack or target the charmer with harmful abilities or spells.<br>**Social Advantage**: Charmer has Advantage on all social ability checks with you. |
| **Deafened** | **Can't Hear**: Auto-fail any ability check requiring hearing. |
| **Exhaustion** | **Cumulative (Levels 1–6)**: Each level inflicts a -2 penalty to all D20 Tests (attack rolls, ability checks, saving throws) and -5 ft Speed.<br>**Death**: Level 6 causes death.<br>**Recovery**: 1 level removed per Long Rest. |
| **Frightened** | **Disadvantage**: Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while source of fear is in line of sight.<br>**Can't Approach**: Cannot willingly move closer to the source of fear. |
| **Grappled** | **Speed 0**: Speed is 0 and cannot increase.<br>**Attacks Affected**: Disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone other than the grappler.<br>**Movable**: Grappler can drag or carry you at half speed unless you are Tiny or 2+ sizes smaller.<br>**Escape**: Action to make Athletics/Acrobatics check vs DC (8 + STR mod + PB). |
| **Incapacitated** | **Inactive**: Cannot take actions, Bonus Actions, or Reactions.<br>**No Concentration**: Concentration is broken immediately.<br>**Speechless**: Cannot speak.<br>**Surprise**: Disadvantage on Initiative rolls. |
| **Invisible** | **Surprise**: Advantage on Initiative rolls.<br>**Concealed**: Cannot be targeted by sight-dependent effects unless seen.<br>**Attacks Affected**: Attacks against you have Disadvantage, your attacks have Advantage. |
| **Paralyzed** | **Incapacitated**: Cannot act or concentrate.<br>**Speed 0**: Speed is 0.<br>**Auto-Fail Saves**: Automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.<br>**Attacks Against**: Attack rolls against you have Advantage.<br>**Auto-Crits**: Any attack roll that hits you is a Critical Hit if attacker is within 5 feet. |
| **Petrified** | **Turned to Stone**: Transformed to solid stone (weight x10, ceases aging).<br>**Incapacitated**: Cannot act or concentrate.<br>**Speed 0**: Speed is 0.<br>**Auto-Fail Saves**: Automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.<br>**Damage Resistance**: Resistance to all damage types.<br>**Poison Immunity**: Immune to Poison damage and the Poisoned condition. |
| **Poisoned** | **Disadvantage**: Disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks. |
| **Prone** | **Restricted Movement**: Can only crawl or spend half Speed (round down) to stand up.<br>**Attacks Affected**: Disadvantage on your attack rolls.<br>**Incoming Attacks**: Attacks within 5 feet have Advantage; attacks from beyond 5 feet have Disadvantage. |
| **Restrained** | **Speed 0**: Speed is 0 and cannot increase.<br>**Attacks Affected**: Attacks against you have Advantage, your attacks have Disadvantage.<br>**Saves Affected**: Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws. |
| **Stunned** | **Incapacitated**: Cannot act or concentrate.<br>**Auto-Fail Saves**: Automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.<br>**Attacks Against**: Attack rolls against you have Advantage. |
| **Unconscious** | **Inert**: Incapacitated and Prone, drops held items. Remains Prone when condition ends.<br>**Speed 0**: Speed is 0.<br>**Auto-Fail Saves**: Automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.<br>**Attacks Against**: Attack rolls against you have Advantage.<br>**Auto-Crits**: Any attack that hits you within 5 feet is an automatic Critical Hit.<br>**Unaware**: Unaware of surroundings. |
