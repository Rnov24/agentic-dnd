# D&D 5e (2024 Revision) Actions & Economy

## Turn Structure
On each combat turn, a creature can:
1. **Move** up to their speed.
2. Take **one Action**.
3. Take **one Bonus Action** (if a feature, spell, or item provides one).
4. Perform **Free Object Interactions** (e.g. drawing a weapon, opening an unlocked door).
5. Take **one Reaction** per round (triggered on another creature's turn).

## Standard Actions
- **Attack**: Make one or more weapon attacks.
- **Cast a Spell**: Cast a spell with a casting time of 1 Action.
- **Dash**: Gain extra movement equal to your speed for the turn.
- **Disengage**: Your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Dodge**: Focus entirely on defense. Attacks against you have Disadvantage; DEX saves have Advantage.
- **Help**: Feint, distract, or assist an ally, granting them Advantage on their next check or attack.
- **Hide**: Make a Stealth check (DC 15) to gain the Invisible condition.
- **Ready**: Specify a trigger and an action to execute as a Reaction.
- **Search**: Make a Perception or Investigation check.
- **Study**: Deduce lore using Arcana, History, Nature, Religion, or Medicine.
- **Utilize**: Manipulate a complex object, activate magic item, or pick a lock.
