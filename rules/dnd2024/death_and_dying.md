# Dropping to 0 HP, Death Saves & Stabilization (D&D Player'\''s Handbook)

This document specifies the rules for dropping to 0 Hit Points, death saving throws, instant death, and stabilization.

---

## 1. Dropping to 0 Hit Points
When a creature drops to 0 hit points, it either dies outright or falls unconscious:
- **Instant Death (Massive Damage)**: When damage reduces you to 0 hit points and there is damage remaining, you die instantly if the remaining damage equals or exceeds your hit point maximum.
  $$\text{Damage Remaining} \ge \text{Max HP} \implies \text{Instant Death}$$
- **Falling Unconscious**: If damage reduces you to 0 hit points and does not kill you outright, you fall **Unconscious**. This ends if you regain any hit points.

---

## 2. Death Saving Throws
Whenever you start your turn with 0 hit points, you must make a special saving throw called a **Death Saving Throw**:
- Roll **1d20** without modifiers:
  - **10 to 19**: **Success** (Record 1 success).
  - **2 to 9**: **Failure** (Record 1 failure).
  - **Natural 20**: **Critical Success** $\rightarrow$ You immediately regain **1 Hit Point** and regain consciousness!
  - **Natural 1**: **Critical Failure** $\rightarrow$ Counts as **2 Failures**!
- **3 Successes**: You become **Stabilized** (remain at 0 HP, unconscious, but no longer make death saves).
- **3 Failures**: You **Die**.
- The number of successes and failures reset to 0 when you regain any hit points or become stabilized.

---

## 3. Damage at 0 Hit Points
- If you take any damage while at 0 hit points, you suffer **1 death saving throw failure**.
- If the damage is from a **critical hit**, you suffer **2 death saving throw failures** instead.
- If the damage equals or exceeds your hit point maximum, you die instantly.

---

## 4. Stabilizing a Creature
- A conscious ally can use an action to administer first aid to an unconscious creature and attempt a **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine)** check.
- On a success, the creature is **Stabilized**.
- A stabilized creature that isn'\''t healed regains 1 hit point after **1d4 hours**.
