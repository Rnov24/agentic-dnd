# Fear & Sanity System (Extended Mechanics)

## Sanity Score & Points
Every character maintains a Sanity Pool (default 100/100).
When exposed to Eldritch entities, horrific transformations, or psychological trauma, the DM requests a **Sanity / Horror Saving Throw** (Wisdom or Charisma).

## Horror Check DCs
- **Mild Dread (DC 10)**: Ghostly whispers, eerie blood writing.
- **Severe Horror (DC 15)**: Witnessing an ally transformed into an aberration, ritual sacrifice.
- **Eldritch Terror (DC 20)**: Glimpsing the Old Gods, madness portals.

## Effects of Sanity Loss
- **100 - 70 Sanity**: Stable.
- **69 - 30 Sanity**: **Shaken**. Unease, disadvantage on Insight and Concentration checks.
- **29 - 1 Sanity**: **Temporary Madness**. Paranoia, hallucinations, -2 penalty to all d20 tests.
- **0 Sanity**: **Permanent Madness**. Catatonic stupor or violent hysteria. Requires Greater Restoration to cure.
