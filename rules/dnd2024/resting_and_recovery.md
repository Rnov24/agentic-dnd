# Resting & Recovery Rules (D&D Player'\''s Handbook)

This document outlines the official resting and recovery mechanics from the Player'\''s Handbook.

---

## 1. Short Rest
- **Duration**: At least 1 hour of downtime (eating, drinking, tending wounds).
- **Hit Dice Recovery**:
  - A character can spend one or more Hit Dice at the end of a short rest.
  - For each Hit Die spent:
    $$\text{HP Regained} = \text{Hit Die Roll} + \text{CON Modifier}$$
  - The player can decide to spend an additional Hit Die after seeing the result of the previous die.
- **Resource Recovery**:
  - Class features that recharge on a short or long rest (e.g. Fighter'\''s *Second Wind*, *Action Surge*, Warlock spell slots) are restored.

---

## 2. Long Rest
- **Duration**: At least 8 hours (at least 6 hours of sleep and no more than 2 hours of light activity).
- **Restrictions**: A character can benefit from no more than one long rest in a 24-hour period, and must have at least 1 hit point at the start of the rest.
- **Effects of Long Rest**:
  - **Hit Points**: All lost hit points are fully restored to max HP.
  - **Hit Dice**: Regain spent Hit Dice up to a number of dice equal to **half of the character'\''s total Hit Dice** (minimum of 1 die).
  - **Spell Slots**: All expended spell slots (1st through 9th level) are fully restored.
  - **Exhaustion**: If the character has levels of Exhaustion, the level is reduced by 1 (provided the character had food and water).
  - **Abilities**: All daily class features, racial traits, and magic item charges with daily resets are refreshed.
