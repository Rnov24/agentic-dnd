# Magic Items (D&D Basic Rules)

This document contains magic item rules, attunement mechanics, and item compendiums imported from the Dungeon Master's Basic Rules.

---

## 1. Magic Item Categories & Rarity

| Rarity | Typical Character Level | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Common** | 1st or higher | Potion of Healing, Spell Scroll (Cantrip, 1st) |
| **Uncommon** | 1st or higher | Bag of Holding, Cloak of Elvenkind, Goggles of Night, Weapon +1, Wand of Magic Missiles |
| **Rare** | 5th or higher | Amulet of Health, Armor +1, Ring of Protection, Ring of Resistance, Potion of Flying |
| **Very Rare** | 11th or higher | Armor +2, Weapon +3, Ring of Evasion, Potion of Invisibility |
| **Legendary** | 17th or higher | Armor +3, Holy Avenger, Sovereign Glue |

---

## 2. Attunement Rules
- A creature can be attuned to no more than **3 magic items** at a time.
- Attuning to an item requires spending a **Short Rest** focused on the item.
- Attunement ends if the creature dies, un-attunes during a short rest, or moves >100 miles away for 24 hours.

## 3. Basic Magic Items Compendium

### Amulet of Health
- *Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*
- Your Constitution score is 19 while wearing this amulet. It has no effect if your Constitution is already 19 or higher.

### Bag of Holding
- *Wondrous item, uncommon*
- Holds up to 500 pounds, not exceeding a volume of 64 cubic feet. The bag always weighs 15 pounds.

### Cloak of Elvenkind
- *Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*
- While wearing this cloak with hood up, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to see you have disadvantage, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide.

### Gauntlets of Ogre Power
- *Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*
- Your Strength score is 19 while wearing these gauntlets.

### Goggles of Night
- *Wondrous item, uncommon*
- While wearing these dark lenses, you gain Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision, wearing the goggles increases its range by 60 feet.

### Headband of Intellect
- *Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*
- Your Intelligence score is 19 while wearing this headband.

### Ring of Protection
- *Ring, rare (requires attunement)*
- You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while wearing this ring.

### Ring of Resistance
- *Ring, rare (requires attunement)*
- You have resistance to one damage type (acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder).

### Wand of Magic Missiles
- *Wand, uncommon*
- Has 7 charges. While holding it, use an action to expend 1 or more charges to cast *Magic Missile* (1 charge = 1st level, +1 slot level per extra charge). Regains 1d6 + 1 charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes.

### Weapon, +1, +2, or +3
- *Weapon, uncommon (+1), rare (+2), or very rare (+3)*
- You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
