# Complete Spellcasting Engine Rules (D&D Player'\''s Handbook)

---

## 1. Anatomy of a Spell
- **Level**: 0 (Cantrip) through 9th level.
- **Casting Time**: 1 Action, 1 Bonus Action, 1 Reaction, or longer (1 minute, 10 minutes, 1 hour).
- **Range**: Self, Touch, ranged (e.g. 30ft, 60ft, 120ft), or Sight.
- **Components**:
  - **Verbal (V)**: Chanted incantations. Cannot cast while gagged or in a *Silence* sphere.
  - **Somatic (S)**: Forceful gestures. Requires at least one free hand.
  - **Material (M)**: Specific objects or arcane focus.
- **Duration**:
  - **Instantaneous**: The magical energy manifests and dissipates immediately.
  - **Concentration**: Requires active focus for up to the duration. Taking damage forces a CON save vs $\max(10, \lfloor \text{damage}/2 \rfloor)$.

---

## 2. Spell Slots & Upcasting
- Casting a spell of 1st level or higher expends a spell slot of that spell'\''s level or higher.
- **Upcasting**: Casting a spell using a slot higher than its base level enhances its potency (e.g. *Magic Missile* adds +1 dart per slot level above 1st; *Fireball* deals +1d6 damage per level above 3rd; *Cure Wounds* heals +1d8 per level above 1st).

---

## 3. Ritual Casting
Certain spells have the **Ritual** tag. A spellcaster with the ritual casting feature can cast such a spell without expending a spell slot by adding **10 minutes** to the normal casting time.
