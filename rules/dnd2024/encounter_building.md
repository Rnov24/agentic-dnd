# Building Combat Encounters (D&D Basic Rules)

This document specifies the official encounter difficulty calculations, XP thresholds, multipliers, and adventuring day pacing from the Dungeon Master's Basic Rules.

---

## 1. Encounter Difficulty Categories

- **Easy**: Doesn't tax the characters' resources or put them in serious peril. They might lose a few HP, but victory is assured.
- **Medium**: One or two scary moments, but characters emerge victorious with no casualties. Might consume healing resources or spell slots.
- **Hard**: Could go badly. Weaker characters might be taken out of the fight, with a slim chance of character death.
- **Deadly**: Lethal for one or more characters. Requires good tactics, cooperation, and quick thinking; party risks defeat.

## 3. Encounter Multipliers for Multiple Monsters

| Number of Monsters | Standard Multiplier (3-5 Party) | Small Party (<3) | Large Party (6+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | $\times 1.0$ | $\times 1.5$ | $\times 0.5$ |
| **2** | $\times 1.5$ | $\times 2.0$ | $\times 1.0$ |
| **3 – 6** | $\times 2.0$ | $\times 2.5$ | $\times 1.5$ |
| **7 – 10** | $\times 2.5$ | $\times 3.0$ | $\times 2.0$ |
| **11 – 14** | $\times 3.0$ | $\times 4.0$ | $\times 2.5$ |
| **15 or more** | $\times 4.0$ | $\times 5.0$ | $\times 3.0$ |

$$\text{Adjusted XP} = \left( \sum \text{Monster Base XP} \right) \times \text{Multiplier}$$

## 5. Situational Modifiers
- **Drawback (increase difficulty by 1 step)**: Party surprised, enemy has superior cover/darkness, environmental hazard hurts party only, restricted mobility.
- **Benefit (decrease difficulty by 1 step)**: Enemy surprised, party has high ground/impenetrable cover, enemy vulnerable to party damage type.
