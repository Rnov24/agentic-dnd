## 3. Special Senses
- **Blindsight**: Perceive surroundings without sight within specified radius (e.g. bats, grimlocks, oozes).
- **Darkvision**: Dim light treated as bright light; darkness treated as dim light (monochrome shades of gray).
- **Tremorsense**: Detect vibrations in ground/substance within radius. Cannot detect flying creatures.
- **Truesight**: See in normal and magical darkness, see invisible creatures, detect visual illusions, see true shape of shapechangers, and perceive the Ethereal Plane.

---

## 4. Legendary Creatures & Lairs
- **Legendary Actions**: Can take 3 special actions per round outside its turn, at the end of another creature's turn. Regains spent actions at start of turn.
- **Lair Actions**: On initiative count 20 (losing all ties), harnesses ambient magic in its lair for powerful area-control effects.
- **Regional Effects**: Ambient magical alterations within 1 to 6 miles of the lair that fade after the creature dies.
