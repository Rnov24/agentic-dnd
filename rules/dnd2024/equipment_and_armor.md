# D&D 5e (2024 Revision) Weapons, Armor, and Equipment

Comprehensive reference based on official 2024 Player's Handbook (Chapter 6).

## 1. Weapon Masteries (D&D 2024)

| Mastery Property | Prerequisite / Weapons | Mechanical Effect (PHB 2024) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleave** | Greataxe, Halberd | If you hit a creature with a melee attack, make a second melee attack roll against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is within reach. Deals weapon damage without ability modifier on hit. |
| **Graze** | Glaive, Greatsword | If your attack roll misses a creature, that creature still takes damage equal to the ability modifier you used for the attack roll (minimum 1). |
| **Nick** | Dagger, Light Hammer, Sickle, Scimitar | When you make the extra attack provided by the Light weapon property, make it as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action (once per turn). |
| **Push** | Greatclub, Pike, Warhammer, Heavy Crossbow | When you hit a creature, push it up to 10 feet straight away from you if it is Large or smaller. |
| **Sap** | Mace, Spear, Flail, Longsword, Morningstar, War Pick | When you hit a creature, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn. |
| **Slow** | Club, Javelin, Light Crossbow, Sling, Whip, Longbow, Musket | When you hit a creature and deal damage, reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. |
| **Topple** | Quarterstaff, Battleaxe, Lance, Maul, Trident | When you hit a creature, force it to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + ability mod + PB) or gain the Prone condition. |
| **Vex** | Handaxe, Dart, Shortbow, Rapier, Shortsword, Blowgun, Pistol | When you hit a creature and deal damage, gain Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn. |
