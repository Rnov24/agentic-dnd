# Lost Mine of Phandelver

**Lost Mine of Phandelver** is an iconic D&D 5e adventure for four to five 1st-level characters, leading them through levels 1–5 in the rugged frontier region of the Sword Coast around Phandalin.

---

## Adventure Background
More than five hundred years ago, clans of dwarves and gnomes made an agreement known as the **Phandelver's Pact**, sharing a rich mine in a wondrous cavern called **Wave Echo Cave**. In addition to vast mineral wealth, the mine contained great magical power channeled through the legendary **Forge of Spells**, allowing human spellcasters allied with the dwarves and gnomes to enchant wondrous magic weapons and items.

Times were good, and the nearby human settlement of **Phandalin** prospered. But catastrophe struck when an army of orcs, reinforced by evil mercenary wizards, swept through the North and laid waste to the region. The battle destroyed Wave Echo Cave, collapsing the tunnels and burying the Forge of Spells beneath tons of rock. The location of the Lost Mine was forgotten for centuries.

Now, the three **Rockseeker brothers** (Gundren, Tharden, and Nundro) have finally uncovered the entrance to Wave Echo Cave. But someone else has learned of their discovery: **Nezznar, the Black Spider**, a ruthless drow mage who has taken control of the local Cragmaw goblin tribe and Redbrand bandit gang to secure the Forge of Spells for himself.

---

## Adventure Structure
- **Part 1: Goblin Arrows (Level 1)**: The heroes are ambushed on the Triboar Trail, track the goblins to the Cragmaw Hideout, rescue Sildar Hallwinter, and discover Gundren Rockseeker has been captured.
- **Part 2: Phandalin (Levels 2–3)**: The party arrives in the frontier town of Phandalin, uncovers local intrigue, confronts the Redbrand Ruffians in their hideout under Tresendar Manor, and unmasks their leader, 'Glasstaff' (Iarno Albrek).
- **Part 3: The Spider's Web (Levels 3–4)**: Wilderness exploration across the Triboar region (Conyberry, Agatha's Lair, Old Owl Well, Thundertree with Venomfang the Green Dragon, Wyvern Tor) leading to the assault on Cragmaw Castle to rescue Gundren and obtain his map.
- **Part 4: Wave Echo Cave (Levels 4–5)**: Infiltration of the Lost Mine, battling undead, the Flameskull, Mormesk the Wraith, discovering the Forge of Spells guarded by a Spectator, and defeating Nezznar the Black Spider.
