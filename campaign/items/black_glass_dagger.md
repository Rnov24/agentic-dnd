# Black Glass Dagger

**Item Type:** Magic Weapon (Dagger)  
**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Attunement:** Required by a Rogue or Shadowblade  
**Damage:** 1d4 + 1 piercing  
**Properties:** Finesse, Light, Thrown (range 20/60)

## Magical Properties
- **Shadow Glint**: Once per short rest, when striking an opponent from hidden concealment, deals an additional +1d6 psychic damage.
- **Muffled Blade**: Attacks made with this dagger produce no auditory resonance.
