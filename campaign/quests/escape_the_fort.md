# Quest: Escape the Fort

**ID:** `escape_the_fort`  
**Status:** Active (Priority: Critical)  
**Giver:** Shadow Syndicate Master  
**Reward:** 500 gp and passage out of the province

## Description
Infiltrate the Blackstone Fortress dungeon, free Valen the Scholar, and escape through the forgotten sewer grate without raising the general alarm.

## Objectives
- [x] Slip past exterior sentries and reach the subterranean dungeon cells
- [x] Unlock or pick the lock of Cell #3 and free prisoner Valen
- [ ] Avoid alerting Captain Aldric and his armored garrison
- [ ] Escape through the southern drainage grate into the whispering woods
