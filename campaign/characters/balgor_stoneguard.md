## Key Features & Tank Synergies with Rodolfo Edinburgh

1. **Origin Feat: Alert (2024 Guard)**:
   - +2 Initiative bonus.
   - **Initiative Swap**: Balgor can immediately swap initiative counts with Rodolfo, ensuring Balgor acts before enemies can reach the wizard.
2. **Fighting Style: Protection**:
   - When an enemy attacks Rodolfo (or another ally within 5 ft), Balgor uses his **Reaction** to impose **Disadvantage** on the enemy's attack roll.
3. **Giant Ancestry: Stone's Endurance**:
   - When Balgor takes damage, he can use his Reaction to roll `1d12 + 2` and reduce the damage taken by that total (2/Long Rest).
4. **Second Wind (2024 Fighter)**:
   - Bonus Action self-heal for `1d10 + 1` HP (2 uses / Short or Long Rest).
5. **Weapon Mastery Peeling**:
   - `Push` with Warhammer allows Balgor to shove melee attackers 10 ft away from Rodolfo without Opportunity Attacks.
   - `Sap` with Flail debuffs dangerous enemies with Disadvantage.

## Personality & Roleplay

- **Personality Traits**: Vigilant, disciplined, and immovable as mountain stone. Constantly checks corners and physical lines of sight that scholars overlook.
- **Ideals**: Guardianship. The brilliant minds who illuminate the world must be shielded by unyielding armor. (Lawful Good)
- **Bonds**: Sworn protector to Rodolfo Edinburgh; bound by sacred oath to keep the fragile scholar alive amidst dungeon perils.
- **Flaws**: Direct and blunt. Impatient with abstract intellectual debates when physical survival is on the line.
