# The Guard Barracks

**Parent Location:** Blackstone Fortress (Ground Level)  
**Commander:** Captain Aldric  
**Threat Level:** High

## Description
A long stone hall filled with double-tiered wooden bunks, weapon racks holding spears, halberds, and heavy crossbows, and a central roaring hearth. At the far end sits Captain Aldric's private command office.

## Key Points of Interest
- **Aldric's Desk**: Contains dispatch orders, garrison rosters, and the Stolen Royal Seal locked in an iron strongbox (DC 16 Sleight of Hand).
- **Armory Rack**: Stocked with chain shirts, heavy shields, and military crossbow bolts.
- **Alarm Gong**: Large bronze bell tied to the central fortress alarm network.
