# The Whispering Woods

**Region:** Northern Wilderness  
**Atmosphere:** Ancient tangled pines, dense moss, perpetual misty shroud.  
**Inhabitants:** Woodland creatures, rebel smugglers, Mira the Alchemist's secluded cottage.

## Secret Outflows
The subterranean drainage ditch of Blackstone Fortress spills into a hidden rocky ravine on the eastern edge of the Whispering Woods, offering an ideal escape path if the party can pick the grate.
