# Blackstone Fortress

**Location Type:** High-Security Military Stronghold  
**Controlling Faction:** Royal Garrison (under Captain Aldric)  
**Atmosphere:** Impenetrable, oppressive dark basalt stone, heavy iron portcullises, rain-slicked battlements.

## Overview
Built two centuries ago atop a jagged crag overlooking the Whispering Woods, Blackstone Fortress serves as the primary garrison and high-security detention center for the northern province.

## Key Sub-Locations
1. **The Dungeon Cells**: Subterranean vaults where high-value prisoners are held in isolation.
2. **The Guard Barracks**: Living quarters, armory, and command desk of Captain Aldric.
3. **The Upper Courtyard**: Open parade ground surrounded by archer towers and alarm bells.
4. **The Lower Drainage Cistern**: Abandoned sewer outflow leading to the river gorge.

## Security Features
- **Alarm Bells**: Rung by sentries if an active breakout is confirmed. Brings 12 heavy guards in 2 rounds.
- **Portcullis System**: Locked via heavy counterweights operated from the guard barracks.
- **Basalt Construction**: Magic-dampening mortar grants resistance against seismic spells.
