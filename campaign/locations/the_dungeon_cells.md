# The Dungeon Cells

**Parent Location:** Blackstone Fortress (Subterranean Level 1)  
**Lighting:** Dim flickering wall sconces with low torches.  
**Sounds:** Constant drip of mineral water, distant rumble of thunder above, quiet murmurs of weary prisoners.

## Tactical Layout (Theater of the Mind)
- **Entrance Corridor**: Heavy reinforced oak door connecting to the spiral stairway leading down from the courtyard.
- **Sentry Post**: A heavy wooden table where Guard Karl is stationed. A ring of iron keys hangs from his leather belt.
- **Cell #1 & #2**: Emptied cells holding rusted chains and damp straw.
- **Cell #3 (Holding Cell)**: Reinforced iron grate where Valen the Scholar is chained.
- **Old Drainage Grate**: Located at the southern wall behind an iron grate (DC 14 Athletics or Sleight of Hand to unlatch).

## Interactive Objects
- **Key Ring on Guard Karl**: Unlocks all cell gates.
- **Torch Sconce**: Can be extinguished to plunge the corridor into complete darkness.
- **Loose Mortar Stone**: Near Cell #3 concealing an old prisoner's shiv (1d4 piercing).
