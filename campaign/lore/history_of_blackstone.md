# History of Blackstone Fortress

Blackstone Fortress was carved out of basalt bedrock during the War of the Four Banners. Originally built as an arcane watchtower to monitor the encroaching darkness of the Whispering Woods, it was fortified into a military redoubt by the Crown.

Deep beneath the lowest prison tiers lie ancient catacombs from the primordial empire, containing forgotten seals and drainage canals.
