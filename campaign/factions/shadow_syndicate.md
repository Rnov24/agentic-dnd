# Faction: The Shadow Syndicate

**Alignment:** Chaotic Good / Freedom Fighters  
**Leader:** The Masked Council  
**Operatives:** Aria Nightwind, Valen the Scholar, Mira the Alchemist

## Objectives
- Expose corrupt nobility and dismantle authoritarian fortresses.
- Rescue captured scholars and couriers.
- Liberate the northern provinces from military extortion.
