# Faction: The Royal Garrison

**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral / Authoritarian  
**Leader:** Captain Aldric (Local), Grand Marshal Vance (Provincial)  
**Headquarters:** Blackstone Fortress

## Objectives
- Maintain martial law and crush rebel resistance across the province.
- Recover contraband artifacts and intercept rebel couriers.

## Resources
- 40 disciplined armored guards
- Fortress siege engines and locked gates
- Direct authority under the Royal Seal
