# Part 4: Wave Echo Cave (Levels 4–5)

## Overview
The Lost Mine of Phandelver! An expansive subterranean complex resonant with the rhythmic booming of underground surf.

## Major Encounters & Hazards
- **Smelter Cavern**: A blazing green flame furnace defended by a **Flameskull** and 8 zombies.
- **Wizard's Quarters**: Haunted by **Mormesk the Wraith**, who guards a chest with arcane scrolls and an enchanted wooden pipe.
- **Forge of Spells**: The legendary magical forge, guarded by a **Spectator** summoned centuries ago who believes the mine is still operational. Houses **Lightbringer** (+1 mace) and **Dragonguard** (+1 breastplate).
- **Temple of Dumathoin**: High-ceilinged temple to the dwarven god of mining. **Nezznar the Black Spider** (drow mage), 4 giant spiders, and 2 doppelgangers. Nezznar carries the *Spider Staff* and his personal journal detailing his quest to claim the Forge.
